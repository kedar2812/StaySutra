There are two roads from Mumbai to Goa. NH-48 through Pune and Belgaum will get
you there in a long day of dual carriageway. NH-66 down the Konkan coast will
take you two, and you will have been somewhere.

## The case for two days

The coast road is roughly 600 kilometres of two-lane road with a hundred river
crossings, and it goes through every fishing town between here and the Goa
border. Ridden in one push it is exhausting and you see none of it. Split over
two days with a night somewhere around Ganpatipule or Malvan, it is one of the
best rides in the country.

## Day one — Mumbai to the middle

Leave before five. This is not optional: the Panvel bottleneck adds an hour after
six on a weekend, and you want to be past Mahad before the day heats up.

The road from Panvel to Mahad is fast and unremarkable. It gets good after
Mahad, where the highway starts weaving between the hills and the sea, and it
gets genuinely beautiful around Guhagar.

**Ferries.** Two of the crossings — Dabhol and Jaigad — are ferries, not
bridges. They run to a schedule that changes with the season and stops
altogether in bad weather. Check both the night before. If either is down the
detour inland costs you two hours.

## Day two — the middle to Goa

Shorter, slower, better. The road tightens between Malvan and the border, the
villages come closer together, and you cross into Goa on a road that does not
feel like a state boundary at all.

Do not push to reach a beach by lunchtime. The interior — Ponda, the Chorla
road, the ferry crossings to Divar and Chorao — is where the riding is, and it is
twenty minutes from anywhere you would want to sleep.

## What the road is actually like

- **Surface:** largely good, with long construction stretches that appear and
  disappear between visits. Expect one bad 20-kilometre section somewhere.
- **Fuel:** frequent enough on the highway, sparse once you turn off it. Fill up
  before any detour to the coast.
- **Traffic:** local, unpredictable, and slower than you. Overtake patiently.
  Buses on this road do not expect you.
- **Night riding:** don't. Unlit, unmarked, and the animals come out.

## When

November to February. May is unbearable. June to September is beautiful and wet
and the ferries are unreliable — doable, but plan for the day you lose.

## The point

NH-48 is a road you use. The coast road is a road you ride. If you have the
extra day, there is no argument.

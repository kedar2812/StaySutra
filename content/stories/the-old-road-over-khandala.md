The Mumbai–Pune Expressway did the old road a favour. It took the trucks, the
buses and most of the cars, and it left the original NH-48 alignment over
Khandala to whoever still wanted it. Two-wheelers are not permitted on the
expressway anyway, so for a rider the choice was made for you — and it turns out
to be the better half of the deal.

## Where it starts

Coming from Mumbai, you leave the new road at Khopoli and climb. Coming from
Pune, you drop out of Lonavala and descend the same stretch in reverse. Either
way it is about eighteen kilometres of continuous gradient with the valley open
on one side and cut rock on the other.

> On a weekday before eight, you will have most of it to yourself. By eleven on a
> Saturday it is a car park with a view.

## What the surface is doing

The tarmac is maintained because the road still carries local traffic and the
occasional overloaded goods carrier that cannot use the expressway. Expect:

- Good surface through most of the climb, with patching after the monsoon
- Gravel wash across the road at three or four of the tighter bends
- One long blind left-hander near the top that people consistently enter too fast

The corners are not technical. They are second and third gear, well cambered, and
the sightlines are honest. What catches people is the traffic mix — you will
share a bend with a cyclist, a monkey and a stopped Innova taking photographs.

## The fog problem

From late June to mid-September the cloud sits on the ghat. It is not a light
mist. Visibility drops to a few metres with no warning, usually in the last hour
of daylight, and it does it in patches — you get clear road, then nothing, then
clear road again.

If you are riding it in the monsoon: go early, keep the visor up and accept the
water, and do not overtake anything you cannot see past. A hi-vis layer does more
for you here than any amount of lighting.

## Where to stop

There is a lay-by roughly two-thirds of the way up on the Mumbai side with a tea
stall that has been there longer than the expressway. It is not signposted and it
is not on any list. You will know it by the six bikes already parked outside.

## Worth knowing

The old road is a legal alternative, not a shortcut — it will cost you thirty to
forty minutes over the expressway. That is the entire point.
